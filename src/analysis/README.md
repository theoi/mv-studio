# src/analysis

Audio intelligence in workers: tempo, beats and downbeats, sections, energy, features, stems, lyrics. Owner: audio area. Results are `AudioAnalysis` / `LyricsDoc` data in `src/model/types.ts`, written through edit commands.

See AGENTS.md for the shared contracts (edit API, job runtime, model loader).

## Song analysis

```ts
import { decodeAudioFile, refinedAnalysisCommand, setAnalysisCommand, SongAnalysisService } from './client.ts'

const service = new SongAnalysisService()
const audio = await decodeAudioFile(file) // main thread (OfflineAudioContext)
const fast = await service.analyze(assetId, audio).promise // job 'analysis.song', a few seconds
editor.dispatch(setAnalysisCommand(fast), { history: 'skip' }) // derived data: no undo step
const refined = await service.refine(assetId).promise // job 'analysis.downbeats', background
editor.dispatch(refinedAnalysisCommand(fast, refined), { history: 'skip' })
```

- **Fast path** (`song.ts` `analyzeSongFast`, permissive DSP): spectral-flux onsets, tempo from autocorrelation with a 120 BPM log-normal prior, dynamic-programming beat tracking, sections, energy and per-frame features. About 2-3 s for a 4-minute song in Chrome. Its bar guesses (bass onsets and harmonic change) place the sections but are not stored: on held-out clips they were right almost never (median downbeat F 0.00), so `downbeats` stays absent until bars are found.
- **Refinement** (`refineSongBeats`): Beat This! (ONNX, WASM threads) beats and downbeats. The beat level is fixed for the whole song, so the beats never switch between half and double time within it: Beat This!'s own level when its intervals agree, otherwise the fast tempo's level. That level can still be wrong for the whole song: on held-out clips the refined tempo was right in 78% and half, double or triple the annotated tempo in another 19%, and the refinement fixed 21 wrong fast tempi but turned 8 right ones wrong. Half and Double in Assist › Song analysis change the level in one click (`setTempoLevel` in `src/app/state/songAnalysis.ts`). Sections are recomputed on the refined bars. `refinedAnalysisCommand` (`analysis.setRefined`) changes the grid and the sections only where the user has not corrected them in the meantime. The worker keeps Beat This!'s output per song, so finding bars again (or for a tapped tempo, `trackAtTempo`) does not run the network twice.
- **Bars** (`AudioAnalysis.downbeats`): only bars that were found, by the refinement or by the user (Set first beat), are stored. They count as found with at least two bar starts (`hasFoundBars` in `src/model/queries.ts`, shared by the bars state, the engine's bar pulse and the reactions). Anything that cuts, snaps or counts on bars reads them through `src/app/state/songAnalysis.ts`: `barsState(project)` / `useBars()` is `ready` (bar starts in timeline time), `finding` (the refinement is running), `unavailable` (it failed, was cancelled, background analysis is off, or no found bars fit the beats the user set) or `none`; until `ready`, fall back to beats. A job the user started that needs bars (auto-cut, the director) awaits `ensureBars({ signal, progress })`, which follows the running refinement or starts one and resolves whether bars were found. `findBarsInBackground()` finds them for a song that has beats but no bars (a project saved before they were found, or a tempo the user changed); `refinedBarsCommand` keeps beats the user set and puts the refined bars on them (`fitDownbeats`). Projects saved with schema version 1 also stored the fast path's guesses; loading drops those (`src/model/migrations.ts`) and the bars are found again.
- **Sections** (`structure/sections.ts`): beat-synchronous chroma and timbre, novelty plus repetition features, boundaries on bar starts, average-linkage grouping into repeat groups, then heuristic labels (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, outro). The chorus itself is found separately as the most repeated loud passage (audio thumbnailing: 4- and 8-bar windows on bars whose beat-by-beat repeats stand out from the song's typical self-similarity, weighted by loudness), and the sections are cut and relabeled to it on downbeats (`withChorus`), so a chorus inside a long section is still found; these chorus sections get `confidence` 0.4 (`AUDIO_CHORUS_CONFIDENCE`). On 62 JamendoLyrics dev songs with a repeated lyric stanza (the reference chorus), chorus precision and recall went from 0.43 and 0.46 to 0.64 and 0.69 (chorus found in 41 songs instead of 27). Labels are editable with `analysis.updateSection`.
- **Features** (`features/features.ts`): 50 Hz loudness (K-weighted) and 7 band envelopes (`BAND_EDGES_HZ`), each 0..1, bands stored frame-major. The song's loud level (90th percentile) maps to 0.9, louder moments are compressed towards 1, and the range below it follows the song's own dynamics (15-40 dB), so compressed masters still swing and quiet sections drop; onset times. The engine samples them with `createAudioSampler`, so preview and export read identical values.
- **Stems** (`stems/`, opt-in): HTDemucs (vocals, drums, bass, other) in its own worker, on WebGPU when available, else WASM threads; the model is a 181 MB download (`STEM_MODEL_BYTES`). `StemSeparationService.estimate(project, songId)` gives the download still needed and the time on this device for the confirmation; `separateSong(songId, { editor, store })` then runs job `analysis.stems` (stages download, decode, separate, features, encode, save) and attaches the stems without an undo step. Underneath, `separate(audio)` returns the stems and `stemEdits(result, song, mediaStore)` stores them as OPFS assets (Opus in Ogg at 48 kHz, else WAV; decoded, each stem starts and ends on the same sample as the song) and returns the `asset.add` and `analysis.setStems` edits that attach `stems` and `stemFeatures`; dispatch them with `{ history: 'skip' }`. A failed allocation in the model is reported as `out-of-memory` (retryable). Stem features are normalized against the mix, so a stem that is absent or quiet stays near 0. Output matches PyTorch demucs at 75-88 dB SDR. On 4-core machines without a GPU (3 WASM threads), a whole job takes 1.2 to 1.6 s per second of song through the app, depending on the machine. The browser's memory peaks 3.3 to 3.5 GB above where it started (3.7 to 3.9 GB in all), which the confirmation rounds up to 4 GB (`STEM_PEAK_MEMORY_BYTES`). The estimate the confirmation shows (`stemJobSeconds`) assumes the slower machine on WASM threads, also on WebGPU, until this device has separated a song of 30 s or more on that device kind. No GPU has been measured, and a GPU session can run parts of the model on WASM. After that first separation, the estimate uses the pace it measured plus 10%, kept in localStorage (`PaceStore`); lyric estimates scale with a WASM pace too. During a run, the job reports the time left from its own pace (`etaSeconds`).
- **Corrections** (`edits.ts`): `rescaleTempo` (double or halve the beat level; found bars stay bars of four through `barsOfFour`: doubling adds a bar start every four new beats, halving leaves none, since which beat of each pair starts a bar is unknown, and none are guessed; the app's Half right after Double restores the bars from before it) and `moveDownbeat` return undoable `analysis.setGrid` edits. Tap tempo: `tapTempo(taps)` (`beats/tap.ts`) gives the BPM of the latest run of taps; `fitTappedTempo(analysis, bpm)` says whether the beats already follow it, follow it at twice or half the level (an instant `rescaleTempo` edit), or must be tracked again: `SongAnalysisService.trackAtTempo(assetId, bpm, file)` (job `analysis.tempo`) refines the period from the audio within 6% of the tapped tempo and returns the grid for one `analysis.setGrid` edit. A re-analysis the user asks for, `analyzeAgain(assetId, file)` (job `analysis.song`: fast pass, then Beat This!, the fast result if Beat This! fails), is a normal dispatch of `setAnalysisCommand`, so undo restores their corrections.

## Lyrics

```ts
import { LyricsService, setLyricsCommand } from './client.ts'

const lyrics = new LyricsService()
// One confirmation before starting: models still to download (names, bytes) and the processing time.
const { downloads, seconds } = await lyrics.estimate(project, songId, { kind: 'time' }) // or { kind: 'draft' }
const { doc, chorus } = await lyrics.run(songId, { kind: 'time', text }, { editor, store: mediaStore })
  .promise
editor.dispatch(setLyricsCommand(doc)) // undoable: the user asked for it
if (chorus) editor.dispatch(chorus, { history: 'skip' }) // sections relabeled from the repeated lines
const dragged = lyrics.realignFrom(doc, lineIndex, wordIndex, time) // no model; undefined after a reload
```

- **One flow on the vocals stem** (`lyrics/client.ts`): job `analysis.lyrics` ("Timing lyrics" or "Drafting lyrics"). A song without stems is separated first in the same job, with one progress bar; the stems are stored right away with `history: 'skip'` and kept even if the lyrics step fails or is cancelled. `estimate()` lists only the downloads still needed (HTDemucs 181 MB when there are no stems, wav2vec2 95 MB, Whisper small 249 MB for drafts) and the time: per second of song, about 1.6 s to separate on 3 WASM threads (`stemJobSeconds`), 0.32 s to time lyrics and 0.74 s to draft them (Whisper hears only the sung passages), plus a few seconds to start (`lyricsSeconds`).
- **Timing pasted lyrics** (`align.ts`, `ctc.ts`, `wav2vec2.ts`, `text.ts`): wav2vec2-base-960h character CTC emissions over the vocals in overlapping windows, then CTC forced alignment (Viterbi) of the pasted words, with a small cost for pauses inside a word. Two more costs keep lines out of instrumental stretches, where separation leaves faint bleed that the model can mistake for singing: a letter or word delimiter placed where `voiceActivity` hears no singing, and a pause between two words of the same line (lines of up to 24 words; lyrics pasted without line breaks get only the first). `scripts/eval-lyric-timing.ts` measures word timing on JamendoLyrics from cached vocals and emissions, with or without them (`--baseline`). Section markers such as "[Chorus]" are dropped; other Latin-script languages are folded to A-Z. On 24 JamendoLyrics songs in four languages, 90.6% of words start within 0.3 s of the reference (86 to 94% per language), with a mean error of 0.31 s. After instrumental stretches of 8 s or more, 83% of the first words land within 0.3 s, up from 50%. On the mix instead of the vocals only about 46% are within 0.3 s, so the flow always uses the vocals.
- **Drafting** (`vad.ts`, `whisper.ts`, `whisperModel.ts`): voice activity on the vocals picks the sung regions, packed into chunks of up to 30 s. Whisper small (Transformers.js, WASM) detects the language on the longest chunks and transcribes each chunk with a token budget proportional to its length, which stops the repetition loops Whisper falls into on singing. The words are then timed with the aligner. Word error rate on the dev songs: 0.48 (Whisper base: 1.1, mostly repetition and invented lines).
- **Corrections**: `realignFrom` pins a dragged word and re-times the words after it on its line; `realignLine` re-times an edited line between its neighbours. Both reuse the aligner output and the voice regions kept for the song during the session (`canRealign`), so the re-timed words stay out of instrumental stretches as the first timing did; after a reload, `prepareRealign(songId, deps)` listens to the vocals again (job `analysis.realign`, about a quarter of the song's length) so they work. `doc.ts` (`groupWordsIntoLines`, `toLyricsDoc`) turns timed words into a `LyricsDoc`.
- **Chorus from lyrics** (`structure/chorus.ts`): choruses repeat their words, verses mostly only their music. `findLyricChorus` takes the passage of at least six words that covers the most of the song: its words times the places it recurs in. An instrumental pause or other words set two places apart; a passage sung again right after itself counts a quarter for each repeat, so a couplet sung four times in an outro does not beat a chorus that comes back across the song. Passages match word by word, tolerating up to two changed, added or dropped words in a row, so the same chorus broken into other lines, pasted as one line, or sung with a few words changed, still matches. The search compares only equal words and bounds its work, so lyrics that chant one word thousands of times still take milliseconds on the main thread. `lyricChorusRanges` combines it with the detected chorus: a detected chorus the lyrics repeat in is confirmed and never shrinks (the lyrics extend it by at most a quarter of its length, both ends together), one the lyrics do not repeat in stays only where mostly repeated words are sung, and a lyric chorus elsewhere takes over the sections it covers most of. `withChorus` cuts the sections at those ranges on downbeats (beats while bars are not found) and labels them chorus with `confidence` 0.8 (`LYRIC_CHORUS_CONFIDENCE`); sections the user named (confidence 1, or a label the analysis never assigns) are never cut, moved or relabeled. `run()` returns it as `chorus`, an `analysis.setRefined` edit that applies only if the sections are unchanged. Only pasted lyrics move the chorus. On the 62 dev songs with a repeated stanza (the reference chorus; `scripts/eval-chorus.ts`), pasted lyrics with their published word times raise chorus precision and recall from 0.63 and 0.70 (audio only, measured the same way) to 0.70 and 0.80, and the chorus is mostly right in 48 to 49 songs instead of 41; it gets better in about 20 songs and worse in 1 to 3 (the ranges cover our measurement and an independent reproduction). Where the lines break does not change the result, even with all the lyrics pasted as one line. The earlier line-by-line matcher reached 0.78 and 0.76 with the published line breaks (worse in 9 songs), 0.78 and 0.53 with the lines broken at pauses instead (worse in 26), and found no lyric chorus in lyrics pasted as one line. Timed by our own aligner (26 of the songs), precision and recall go from 0.60 and 0.62 to 0.71 and 0.78 (better in 10 songs, worse in none; the line matcher: 0.73 and 0.74, worse in 3). Measured on 19 songs with the earlier matcher, Whisper small drafts (word error rate 0.48) would lower them to 0.53 and 0.29: their repeated runs are short and partial. So a draft only confirms the detected chorus, raising its confidence to 0.6 (`DRAFT_CONFIRMED_CHORUS_CONFIDENCE`), when its own chorus mostly lies inside it: in 9 of those songs, where the audio chorus precision is 0.66, against 0.40 in the 5 where the draft's chorus lies elsewhere.

## Evaluation data

`scripts/fetch-analysis-fixtures.sh [en de fr es]` downloads the JamendoLyrics songs (Creative Commons, some NC or ND, never commit them) with word-level lyric timings into `tests/fixtures/.cache/jamendolyrics/`, pinned to a dataset revision and checked against `scripts/analysis-fixtures.sha256`. `ANALYSIS_BENCH=12 npx playwright test tests/e2e/analysis-bench.spec.ts` then times decoding, fast analysis and refinement on that many songs in Chromium; `ANALYSIS_BENCH_STEMS=1` adds stem separation and `ANALYSIS_BENCH_LYRICS=align` (or `base`, `small` for drafts) the lyrics flow with word onset errors.
